Timely Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Timely is a personal planner app used by its developer only. It has no servers. All processing happens on the user's own devices, and data is stored only on those devices and in the user's own Google account.

Data Timely accesses

  • Google Calendar: the list of your calendars and their events, read so that tasks can be scheduled around them.
  • Timely calendar: Timely creates one calendar named "Timely" and creates, updates and deletes events only in that calendar. It cannot modify events in any other calendar.
  • Google Drive (when sync is enabled): only files and folders that Timely itself creates, used to sync your tasks between your devices and to store backups and logs.
  • Your tasks, notes and usage logs: created by you in the app and stored on your devices.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide Timely's features: scheduling, showing your calendar, syncing between your own devices, and analysing your own planning history. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with third parties, and never transferred to anyone other than you.

Where data is stored

  • On your devices, in an encrypted local database.
  • In your own Google Drive, encrypted on the device before upload (attachments may be kept readable at your choice).
  • In your own Google Calendar, as events in the Timely calendar.

The developer operates no server and receives no copy of your data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Timely's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Revoke Timely's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the "Timely" calendar in Google Calendar and the "Timely" folder in Google Drive to remove everything Timely stored there.
  • Uninstall the app to delete local data from a device.
