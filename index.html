Timely

A personal calendar and task planner for Android and Windows.

Timely keeps a list of tasks with estimates and deadlines, places them as time blocks around the events in your Google Calendar, and reschedules automatically when plans change.

It is a private, single-user app. It runs entirely on your own devices and talks directly to Google's APIs with your permission. There is no Timely server, no account with us, and no data shared with anyone.
